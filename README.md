# Digital Marketing Data Analysis Project
### Project Overview
This project analyzes 160,000+ records of marketing campaign data for three major brands (Nykaa, Purplle, Tira). The goal was to build a data pipeline and interactive dashboard to track Net Profit, Cost Per Conversion, and Return on Investment (ROI) across various marketing channels.
### Tools Used 
**Python (Pandas)**: Data cleaning and combining multiple raw CSV files into a master dataset.

**PostgreSQL**: Writing advanced queries (CTEs, Window Functions, Funnel Analysis) to extract business insights.

**Power BI**: Building an interactive executive dashboard using custom DAX measures (Net Profit, Profit Margin).

### Key Business Insights Discovered
1. **The market is perfectly tied**: All three brands (Nykaa, Purplle, and Tira) are making almost the exact same amount of money. The market is split evenly into perfect thirds (around 33% each), meaning no single brand is dominating the competition.
2. **Email is the top earner**: Even though video and social media are popular, plain old Email marketing actually brings in the most money. Instagram and Facebook are close behind, but YouTube sits at the bottom of the list.
3. **Getting a customer is incredibly cheap**: The campaigns are highly profitable. It costs less than a single dollar (just 0.37) in marketing spend to get one customer to actually buy something.
4. **Sales are very steady**: Daily sales stayed very consistent for almost a year without any crazy ups and downs. The sharp drop-off at the very end of the line chart isn't a crash in sales; it just means the data collection was cut off before that final month finished.
